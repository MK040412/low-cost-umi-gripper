## Report Template

All technical reports follow a standard template:

1. **Overview** - Brief description
2. **Background** - Context and motivation
3. **Methodology** - Approach and methods
4. **Results** - Findings and data
5. **Analysis** - Discussion of results
6. **Conclusions** - Summary and future work
7. **References** - Citations and resources

# Bill of Materials

Complete list of parts needed to build the Low-Cost UMI Gripper.

## Summary

| Category | Estimated Cost |
|----------|---------------|
| Mechanical | $15-25 |
| Electronics | $20-35 |
| Sensors | $10-20 |
| Miscellaneous | $5-10 |
| **Total** | **$50-90** |

## Mechanical Parts

| Part | Quantity | Est. Cost | Notes |
|------|----------|-----------|-------|
| 3D Printed Frame | 1 set | $5-10 | PLA or PETG |
| M3 Screws (various) | ~30 | $3 | Assorted lengths |
| M3 Nuts | ~20 | $2 | |
| Bearings (608ZZ) | 4 | $3 | 8x22x7mm |
| Springs | 2 | $2 | Tension springs |

## Electronics

| Part | Quantity | Est. Cost | Notes |
|------|----------|-----------|-------|
| ESP32 DevKit | 1 | $5-8 | Or Arduino Nano |
| Servo Motor (MG996R) | 2 | $8-12 | High torque |
| Motor Driver | 1 | $3-5 | L298N or similar |
| Power Supply (5V 3A) | 1 | $5-8 | |
| Wires & Connectors | - | $3-5 | Assorted |

## Sensors

| Part | Quantity | Est. Cost | Notes |
|------|----------|-----------|-------|
| FSR 402 | 2-4 | $5-10 | Force sensors |
| AS5600 Encoder | 1-2 | $3-6 | Magnetic encoder |
| USB Camera | 1 | $10-20 | Optional |

## Tools Required

- 3D Printer (or access to printing service)
- Soldering iron
- Screwdriver set
- Multimeter
- Wire strippers

## Where to Buy

- Electronics: AliExpress, Amazon, Adafruit, SparkFun
- Hardware: Local hardware store, McMaster-Carr
- 3D Printing: Print yourself or use services like JLCPCB, Craftcloud

## Notes

- Prices are estimates and may vary by region
- Generic parts can be substituted for brand names
- Buy extras for spares and experimentation

# Hardware Guide

This guide covers the hardware components and assembly of the Low-Cost UMI Gripper.

## Overview

The hardware module contains designs and specifications for building an affordable gripper mechanism.

## Components

### Mechanical Parts
- Gripper frame (3D printed)
- Finger mechanisms
- Mounting brackets

### Actuators
- Servo motors
- Stepper motors (optional)

### Electronics
- Microcontroller (Arduino/ESP32)
- Motor drivers
- Power supply
